Summary

One late-stage asset carries the story; the amylin half is early and thinly disclosed

BGM0504
GLP-1R / GIPR dual agonist · weekly SC · oral tablet in Phase 2
NDA under review in China (accepted 12 Jun 2026)
−19.2%body weight at 52 weeks on 15 mg versus −3.1% on placebo (Phase 3, N = 652)
  • Tirzepatide-like PK in humans: t½ 109–160 h, Tmax about 24 h.
  • Cross-trial, weight loss is level with or ahead of tirzepatide in Chinese patients; no head-to-head result is public.
  • GI event rates for Phase 3 are not disclosed. Diarrhoea reached 75% at 15 mg in the T2D Phase 2a.
BGM1812
Amylin / calcitonin receptor agonist · weekly SC, possibly every two weeks
Phase 1 complete (CN, US) · Phase 2 ongoing
−8.4% to −9.2%best placebo-adjusted weight loss after 1–4 weekly doses, no titration (Phase 1, 60 subjects per country)
  • About 2× petrelintide potency in vitro, soluble above 66 mg/mL at pH 7.4, no fibrils at pH 6.5–7.5.
  • Rat PK matches petrelintide; human half-life is not published by the company.
  • No per-dose human data, no adverse-event rates, no toxicology.
BGM2102
Fixed-dose combination of BGM0504 + BGM1812 · SC
Preclinical · IND being prepared (CN, US)
−28%body weight in DIO rats versus −15% and −13% for the single agents (one conference abstract)
  • Both peptides are soluble at neutral pH, so a single-chamber product is plausible. No co-formulation data are public.
  • Component half-lives differ (rat 14 h vs 21 h); dose ratio, PK interaction and toxicology are undisclosed.
  • Outside the China Resources Sanjiu licence, so rights are unencumbered.

What a reviewer should take away

  • BGM0504 is a credible tirzepatide follower. Potency, protein binding, animal PK and human PK all sit close to tirzepatide. The 52-week result (−19.2%) is the strongest disclosed for a GLP-1/GIP agonist in a Chinese population, with the caveat that the estimand is undisclosed.
  • Tolerability is the open question. Phase 2 vomiting at 15 mg (31%) is above tirzepatide's in SURMOUNT-CN (20%), and titration steps were two weeks rather than four. Phase 3 discontinuation for adverse events was low (0.7–1.7%), which argues against a serious problem.
  • The amylin programme rests on one paper and two company notices. BGM1812's preclinical package is sound but small (n = 3 rat PK, one DIO study). The Phase 1 notice gives ranges only.
  • BGM2102 is a concept with one data point. The −28% figure comes from an abstract without N, duration or statistics, and its BGM1812 dose (0.4 mg/kg) is inconsistent with the same abstract's dose range.
  • Formulation is where the assets are most distinctive for us. BGM0504 is formulated at pH 7.2–7.6 in propylene glycol without NaCl; BGM1812 is stable at neutral pH. That avoids the pH mismatch that forces CagriSema into a dual-chamber pen.
  • Corporate risk is real. The Hong Kong listing was terminated on 30 Sep 2026, the controlling shareholder has been on bail since June 2026, and the US Phase 3 (estimated RMB 2.1bn) is unfunded. Ex-China rights appear to be available.

BGM0504 · molecule and preclinical

A tirzepatide-derived peptide with the lipid moved to a new C-terminal lysine

BGM0504 keeps tirzepatide's 39-residue backbone, adds a lysine at position 40 and moves the fatty-diacid side chain from Lys20 to Lys40. The compound patent family (WO2022199629, priority March 2021) covers it; the US patent was granted in February 2025 and should run to March 2042.12

Titration is the likely driver. BGM0504 trials stepped the dose every two weeks (2.5 → 5 → 10 → 15 mg); tirzepatide steps every four. BGM1812 · amylin / calcitonin receptor agonist

A petrelintide analogue with about twice the potency and the same neutral-pH behaviour

BGM1812 came out of three rounds of structure-guided optimisation starting from petrelintide, using two methylation strategies. Formula C182H299N49O61, MW 4,149.6. The sequence is not public; a 2026 BrightGene patent describes 37-residue analogues with a 2–7 lactam bridge, a C20 diacid–γGlu side chain and α-methyl residues at positions 12 and 15.713

Optimisation series: BGM1812 is the only analogue clearly better at both receptors

Why the formulation logic holds together

  • BGM0504 is formulated at pH 7.2–7.6; BGM1812 is soluble and fibril-free at pH 6.5–7.5. The windows overlap at roughly pH 7.2–7.5.
  • Cagrilintide needs pH 3.5–4.5 and semaglutide needs pH above about 5.9, so CagriSema uses two chambers through one needle.24
  • Petrelintide showed no extra degradation when co-formulated with semaglutide at pH 6.7–7.4, which is the closest public precedent.25
  • Unknowns specific to this pair: BGM0504 loses solubility in NaCl (0.15–1 mg/mL), so tonicity must come from propylene glycol; whether BGM1812 tolerates that vehicle, and a preservative, is untested in public.

Why no salt

BGM0504 dissolves above 30 mg/mL in propylene glycol but only 0.15 mg/mL (first patent) to about 1 mg/mL (second patent) in NaCl, with aggregation. Tirzepatide tolerates NaCl. Moving the lipid to Lys40 appears to have changed the salt sensitivity.

Stability

Stable for one month at 2–8 °C. At 40 °C, purity fell from 93.34% to 89.97% in 10 days; the peptide is heat- and light-sensitive. No long-term, in-use or room-temperature excursion data are public. Starting purity of 93% is low for a commercial peptide and worth asking about.

Injection volume

At the 20 mg/mL example, 2.5–15 mg would be 0.125–0.75 mL. An independent claim covering 5–30 mg/mL matches 2.5–15 mg in a fixed 0.5 mL, the tirzepatide design. The commercial choice is not disclosed. Inference

No depot work is visible. No BrightGene patent or programme for a microsphere, in-situ gel or other sustained-release form of BGM0504 or BGM1812 was found. The company's route to longer intervals is molecular (BGM1962, siRNA). The process patent (WO2025092769) reports crude purity of 66–73% for the solid-phase route.

Shape only: concentrations are normalised to the steady-state peak, so dose and bioavailability cancel out. When absorption is slower than elimination the curve is release-limited (flip-flop), which is how a depot flattens the profile. This is an illustration, not a prediction for any product.

Reading for BGM0504. As a solution it is a weekly drug: at two weeks the swing is about 5×, at four weeks above 25×.
