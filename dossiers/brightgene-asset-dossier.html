A long-acting BrightGene asset: worth doing, possible, and with which molecule

The planning premise is that Daewoong applies a long-acting formulation to BGM0504, BGM1812 or BGM2102. This page works that decision end to end: how good the three assets are, whether a longer interval is even feasible, and which molecule and platform to pair.

Bottom line

Four conclusions, before the detail

The extended-interval race has already moved past formulation: nearly every monthly rival got there by changing the molecule — a second lipid, an antibody — not by putting a weekly molecule in a depot. A depot is still worth building, but only on three conditions, and they point to one molecule.

BGM0504 is the strong asset

−19.2% at 52 weeks, tirzepatide-like PK, NDA under review in China. But too potent-per-milligram to make monthly: only the 5 mg dose fits a depot, and it is salt-insoluble.

BGM1812 is the right one to make long-acting

A low-dose (0.3–7.2 mg) amylin agonist — the only BrightGene molecule whose payload fits a depot or a microneedle patch. Thinly disclosed, so data is the gate.

BGM2102 is a concept, and already contested

One rat abstract (−28%, additive not synergistic). A competitor (Ascletis) already has the same combination concept in the clinic, monthly.

Position it as maintenance

The one clinical depot (ASC30) traded depth of effect for tolerability and durability. A depot's win is adherence, not percentage lost.

Can we make it long-acting · the ceiling

Weekly does not simply become monthly

Both BrightGene injectables ride on albumin: a C20 diacid borrows albumin's roughly one-week turnover. That is a ceiling, not a dial — more lipid buys no more time. Every monthly rival got past it by using a bigger carrier (an antibody) or a slower outlet (a depot).

Solubility is the harder wall. BGM0504 is nearly insoluble in water and falls to 1.04 mg/mL in saline; reaching even 20 mg/mL needs propylene glycol at pH 7.2–7.6.11 So BGM0504 fits only at 5 mg — which delivered 14.3% in Phase 3, not 19.2%. BGM1812's whole dose range fits comfortably.
Can we make it long-acting · the trade

What a depot buys, what it costs, and the chemistry traps

One depot in this class has human efficacy data: Ascletis' ASC30, a 24-week Phase 2 with three monthly doses and no titration. It states the trade plainly.

Placebo-adjusted weight loss: depot vs long-acting molecules

Company toplines, different trials and time points. Company4952

Buys

  • Tolerability no weekly product matches — every GI event grade 1, zero adverse-event discontinuations
  • Durability — 5.8% held four months after the last dose
  • No titration needed

Costs

  • Depth: 6.3% vs 11–14% for weekly/monthly molecules
  • Formulation risk: 1 of 2 ASC30 formulations missed exposure
  • Cannot be titrated or reversed inside an interval

BGM0504 in PLGA: the Lys20 trap

PLGA's acidic degradation acylates free amines. BGM0504 was designed with its lipid on Lys40, leaving Lys20 free — a month in a PLGA depot could re-acylate Lys20 and undo the potency gain the molecule was built for.43

Cheap screen: incubate in degrading PLGA, check Lys20 by LC-MS. If it fails, use a neutral hydrogel instead.

BGM1812 at concentration: aggregation

Amylins fibrillate and dimerise, both concentration-driven (an analogue in Zealand's work hit 42% dimer at 20 mg/mL). A depot needs ~4× the weekly dose in the same volume. BGM1812's anti-fibrillation claim rests on α-methylation, but no quantitative data is published.29

Cheap screen: thioflavin-T and SEC at 1 and 20 mg/mL under the petrelintide conditions.

The plan

Pair the molecule to the platform, and lead with BGM1812

The cleanest fit in the analysis. If the Daewoong platform in scope is the microneedle patch (a few milligrams), lead with BGM1812: its entire range is 0.3–7.2 mg, while BGM0504's top dose is six times the semaglutide load the patch was built for. For a depot, BGM1812 or BGM0504 at the 5 mg equivalent — never a 15 mg-equivalent monthly BGM0504.

Ask BrightGene, in order

  1. BGM1812's measured half-life and BGM0504's solubility ceiling — these decide whether there is a programme.
  2. BGM1812's accumulation ratio and BGM0504's absolute SC bioavailability.
  3. A single-assay potency head-to-head (the 1.8×/2.2× claims use an in-house comparator).
  4. The full ADA 2025 BGM2102 poster — N, schedule, statistics.
  5. Their answer to Ascletis' ASC36_35FDC, already in the clinic monthly.

Do in parallel

  • Run the two cheap screens — PLGA acylation on BGM0504, fibrillation on BGM1812 — before choosing a platform.
  • Frame the product as maintenance, not induction — it removes the titration problem instead of engineering around it.
  • Freedom-to-operate on the Korean microsphere players (Peptron, Inventage, G2G Bio) and Ascletis' self-assembling depot.
  • Decide what it wins on besides interval — Pfizer is monthly by molecule, orforglipron is oral, BGM0504's own tablet is in Phase 2.

Why the formulation logic holds

  • BGM0504 at pH 7.2–7.6 and BGM1812 fibril-free at pH 6.5–7.5 overlap at ≈7.2–7.5 — a single-chamber product is plausible, unlike CagriSema's dual chamber.24
  • Component half-lives differ (rat 14 h vs 21 h), so a single depot would need component-specific release rates.
  • Unknown: whether BGM1812 tolerates the propylene-glycol, preservative vehicle BGM0504 needs.

Status: IND being prepared (CN, US); no clearance, registry entry or first-in-human date. Dose ratio, co-formulation and toxicology undisclosed.38
