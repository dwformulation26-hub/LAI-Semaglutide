Amylin / calcitonin receptor agonist · weekly SC, possibly every two weeks

BGM2102
Fixed-dose combination of BGM0504 + BGM1812 · SC
Preclinical · IND being prepared (CN, US)
−28%body weight in DIO rats versus −15% and −13% for the single agents (one conference abstract)
  • Both peptides are soluble at neutral pH, so a single-chamber product is plausible. No co-formulation data are public.
  • Component half-lives differ (rat 14 h vs 21 h); dose ratio, PK interaction and toxicology are undisclosed.
  • Outside the China Resources Sanjiu licence, so rights are unencumbered.

What a reviewer should take away

  • BGM0504 is a credible tirzepatide follower. Potency, protein binding, animal PK and human PK all sit close to tirzepatide. The 52-week result (−19.2%) is the strongest disclosed for a GLP-1/GIP agonist in a Chinese population, with the caveat that the estimand is undisclosed.
  • Tolerability is the open question. Phase 2 vomiting at 15 mg (31%) is above tirzepatide's in SURMOUNT-CN (20%), and titration steps were two weeks rather than four. Phase 3 discontinuation for adverse events was low (0.7–1.7%), which argues against a serious problem.
  • The amylin programme rests on one paper and two company notices. BGM1812's preclinical package is sound but small (n = 3 rat PK, one DIO study). The Phase 1 notice gives ranges only.
  • BGM2102 is a concept with one data point. The −28% figure comes from an abstract without N, duration or statistics, and its BGM1812 dose (0.4 mg/kg) is inconsistent with the same abstract's dose range.
  • Formulation is where the assets are most distinctive for us. BGM0504 is formulated at pH 7.2–7.6 in propylene glycol without NaCl; BGM1812 is stable at neutral pH. That avoids the pH mismatch that forces CagriSema into a dual-chamber pen.
  • Corporate risk is real. The Hong Kong listing was terminated on 30 Sep 2026, the controlling shareholder has been on bail since June 2026, and the US Phase 3 (estimated RMB 2.1bn) is unfunded. Ex-China rights appear to be available.

BGM0504 · molecule and preclinical

A tirzepatide-derived peptide with the lipid moved to a new C-terminal lysine

BGM0504 keeps tirzepatide's 39-residue backbone, adds a lysine at position 40 and moves the fatty-diacid side chain from Lys20 to Lys40. The compound patent family (WO2022199629, priority March 2021) covers it; the US patent was granted in February 2025 and should run to March 2042.12

BGM1812 · amylin / calcitonin receptor agonist

A petrelintide analogue with about twice the potency and the same neutral-pH behaviour

BGM1812 came out of three rounds of structure-guided optimisation starting from petrelintide, using two methylation strategies. Formula C182H299N49O61, MW 4,149.6. The sequence is not public; a 2026 BrightGene patent describes 37-residue analogues with a 2–7 lactam bridge, a C20 diacid–γGlu side chain and α-methyl residues at positions 12 and 15.713

Why the formulation logic holds together

  • BGM0504 is formulated at pH 7.2–7.6; BGM1812 is soluble and fibril-free at pH 6.5–7.5. The windows overlap at roughly pH 7.2–7.5.
  • Cagrilintide needs pH 3.5–4.5 and semaglutide needs pH above about 5.9, so CagriSema uses two chambers through one needle.24
  • Petrelintide showed no extra degradation when co-formulated with semaglutide at pH 6.7–7.4, which is the closest public precedent.25
  • Unknowns specific to this pair: BGM0504 loses solubility in NaCl (0.15–1 mg/mL), so tonicity must come from propylene glycol; whether BGM1812 tolerates that vehicle, and a preservative, is untested in public.

Why no salt

BGM0504 dissolves above 30 mg/mL in propylene glycol but only 0.15 mg/mL (first patent) to about 1 mg/mL (second patent) in NaCl, with aggregation. Tirzepatide tolerates NaCl. Moving the lipid to Lys40 appears to have changed the salt sensitivity.

Stability

Stable for one month at 2–8 °C. At 40 °C, purity fell from 93.34% to 89.97% in 10 days; the peptide is heat- and light-sensitive. No long-term, in-use or room-temperature excursion data are public. Starting purity of 93% is low for a commercial peptide and worth asking about.

Injection volume

At the 20 mg/mL example, 2.5–15 mg would be 0.125–0.75 mL. An independent claim covering 5–30 mg/mL matches 2.5–15 mg in a fixed 0.5 mL, the tirzepatide design. The commercial choice is not disclosed. Inference

No depot work is visible. No BrightGene patent or programme for a microsphere, in-situ gel or other sustained-release form of BGM0504 or BGM1812 was found. The company's route to longer intervals is molecular (BGM1962, siRNA). The process patent (WO2025092769) reports crude purity of 66–73% for the solid-phase route.

Why weekly does not simply become monthly

Both BrightGene injectables get their duration from a C20 diacid that binds albumin reversibly, so the peptide rides on albumin's turnover instead of its own. That is a ceiling, not a dial: once the molecule tracks albumin, more lipid adds no more time.

In bioreactor terms the molecule already matches the residence time of the vessel. Going longer needs a bigger vessel (an antibody recycled by FcRn) or a slower outlet (a reservoir that releases more slowly than the body eliminates).

Where the ceiling sits depends on the peptide. Singly lipidated GLP-1 and GIP agonists stop at 5–7 days. Singly lipidated amylins already reach 10–14 days in humans, and dual lipidation takes both classes to 16–19 days, close to albumin itself. Nothing that relies on albumin goes further.

BGM1812's half-life has never been disclosed as a number. The Phase 1 notice, both registry entries and the paper say only that it supports weekly or every-two-week dosing. A BrightGene patent reports 274–363 h for an unnamed analogue. Every long-acting decision for this molecule depends on that number, so it is the first thing to ask for.

Long-acting strategy · clinical depot evidence

A depot buys tolerability and durability and gives up depth of effect

Ascletis ran a 24-week, double-blind, placebo-controlled US Phase 2 of its ASC30 subcutaneous depot in 65 adults: three 400 mg doses on days 1, 29 and 57 with no weekly lead-in, then no further dosing to week 24. One of the two formulations tested never reached therapeutic exposure; the other (A1) is shown here.4950

Placebo-adjusted weight loss: the depot against long-acting molecules

Company toplines from different trials and time points. Company49525342

A second depot has human weight data without a placebo arm: Camurus's monthly semaglutide depot CAM2056 lost 9.3% at day 85 against 5.2% on weekly semaglutide in a Phase 1b.36 That result is for a marketed peptide and points the other way on depth of effect, so the ASC30 trade-off may be specific to that product.

What the depot bought

  • Tolerability no weekly product matches. Every GI event was grade 1. No discontinuation for adverse events in any arm. No hepatic or ECG signal.
  • Durability. Weight loss peaked at 7.5% two months after the last dose and was still 5.8% four months after it.
  • No titration. It did this without a weekly lead-in, the thing a depot is supposed to be bad at.

What it cost

  • Depth of effect. 6.3% at the week-12 primary, against 11–14% for the weekly and monthly molecules.
  • Formulation risk. One of two formulations failed on exposure in humans.
  • Small and company-reported. n = 65, topline only, no per-arm sizes or confidence intervals.
The design brief that follows. A depot in this class is a way to make a weight-loss drug easier to stay on, not stronger. That points to positioning a long-acting BrightGene asset as maintenance after titration on the weekly product, which is also how BrightGene frames BGM2102. Ascletis has already taken that concept into the clinic: ASC36_35FDC, a monthly amylin plus a monthly GLP-1/GIP agonist in one injection, has an FDA IND and has started Phase 1.48
Long-acting strategy · payload

Long-acting strategy · technical risks

Each molecule has its own chemistry trap, and a depot cannot be titrated

BGM0504 in PLGA: the Lys20 question

PLGA degrades into acidic oligomers that build up inside the particle and acylate accessible primary amines on the peptide. Octreotide released from PLGA microspheres was only about 25% unmodified over 95 days, and exenatide showed progressive pH-dependent acylation in rats.43

BGM0504 exists because its fatty acid was moved off Lys20 and onto Lys40, since acylating Lys20 costs receptor potency. A PLGA depot would hold a free, reactive Lys20 in an acylating environment for a month, and could undo the very potency gain the molecule was designed for.

Cheap test: incubate in a degrading PLGA film or blank microspheres and screen for glycolic-acid adducts by LC-MS, checking Lys20 specifically. Leuprolide, which has no free primary amine, showed none at 28 days, so this is a decisive, inexpensive experiment. If it fails, move off polyester: a more hydrophilic polyester (PLHMGA) raised unmodified octreotide recovery above 65–70%, and a polymer-nanoparticle hydrogel held both tirzepatide and semaglutide for 43 days in rats with no burst, through a 21G needle.41

BGM1812 at concentration: aggregation

Amylin analogues fibrillate and dimerise, and both grow with concentration. In Zealand's petrelintide work an intermediate analogue formed 27% dimer at 1 mg/mL and 42% at 20 mg/mL after two weeks at 40 °C, pH 7.0.29

A depot needs about four times the weekly dose in the same or less volume, which puts the molecule into that concentration range. BGM1812's anti-fibrillation claim rests on α-methylation at Leu12 and Phe15, which is sound chemistry, but no quantitative fibrillation or dimerisation data has been published.

Cheap test: run thioflavin-T and size-exclusion chromatography at 1 and 20 mg/mL, two weeks at 40 °C, pH 7.0, mirroring the petrelintide conditions so the numbers are comparable.

Handle one potency claim with care. BrightGene reports BGM1812 at AMY3R 0.627 nM against its own petrelintide value of 1.126 nM and calls that 1.8× more potent. Zealand's published value for petrelintide is AMY3R 0.33 nM. Different assays, so not directly comparable, but an independent lab measures petrelintide as more potent than BrightGene measures BGM1812. The 1.8× and 2.2× claims rest on BrightGene's in-house comparator; do not repeat them without that caveat, and ask for a single-assay head-to-head.29

A depot cannot be titrated, and that decides the positioning

GI events in this class track dose and escalation speed. Every weekly product manages them with titration. A depot cannot be titrated within an interval and cannot be withdrawn if a patient reacts.

The cleanest technical fit in this whole analysis. If the Daewoong technology in scope is the microneedle patch, lead with BGM1812, not BGM0504. The patch carries a few milligrams; BGM1812's entire clinical range is 0.3–7.2 mg; BGM0504's top dose is more than six times the semaglutide dose the patch was built around. BGM1812 is also the molecule where a weekly-to-biweekly or monthly claim is already in the company's narrative, and it had no grade 3 or serious adverse events in its Chinese Phase 1.

What to do, in order

  • Ask BrightGene for two numbers: BGM1812's measured half-life and BGM0504's solubility ceiling in their own system. They decide whether there is a programme at all. Add BGM1812's accumulation ratio and BGM0504's absolute SC bioavailability.
  • Run the PLGA acylation screen on BGM0504 before choosing a platform. Cheap, fast, and a positive result rules out the obvious platform and saves a year.
  • Measure BGM1812's fibrillation and dimerisation at 1 and 20 mg/mL under the petrelintide conditions, so the numbers are comparable to published work.
  • Frame the target product as maintenance, not induction. Every depot datapoint says so, and it removes the titration problem instead of engineering around it.
  • Pair molecule to platform on payload. Microneedle → BGM1812. Depot → BGM0504 at the 5 mg equivalent, or BGM1812. Do not promise a 15 mg-equivalent monthly BGM0504 depot.
  • Get a freedom-to-operate opinion covering Peptron, G2G Bio and Inventage before a microsphere route in Korea, and ask what IP position Ascletis's self-assembling depot creates.
  • Put ASC36_35FDC to BrightGene and ask for their answer: a competitor has the BGM2102 concept in the clinic, monthly, while BGM2102 is preclinical and weekly. Their response tests their own timeline.
  • Decide what the product wins on besides interval. Pfizer reaches monthly with a molecule, orforglipron is oral, and BGM0504's own tablet is in Phase 2. Interval alone is no longer a differentiator.
